The app is organized under `Frontend/studentbite/` using the Next.js App Router with three sibling route groups: `login`, `register`, and `onboarding` pages handle unauthenticated entry; `(main)/` wraps authenticated tabs (home, planner, stores, history) behind a `TabBar`; and a root `app/layout.tsx` injects a `Providers` component that bootstraps TanStack Query, form context, and global styles. All pages share data through `lib/api.ts` (TanStack Query client), TypeScript schemas/types in `lib/types.ts`, and the user-auth hook from `lib/hooks.ts`. Reusable UI lives in `components/` (navigation, progress indicators, map, alerts) and is imported via the `@/` alias. The mobile-first layout caps content at 480px and is enforced consistently across all routes.
