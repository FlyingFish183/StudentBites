### StudentBites
- Definition：A student-focused meal planning and grocery shopping optimization application that helps students plan meals, track nutrition, find nearby stores, and compare prices across different retailers.
- Aliases：SB、studentbite

### Thực đơn
- Definition：Meal plan/schedule for a specific date, containing multiple dishes across different meal types (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack) with nutritional information and estimated costs.
- Aliases：meal plan、menu

### Nguyên liệu
- Definition：Basic food ingredients with nutritional data (protein, carbs, fat, kcal per 100g) and keywords for product matching during web crawling.
- Aliases：ingredient、food item

### Cửa hàng
- Definition：Retail locations including supermarkets, convenience stores, traditional markets, and online stores where students can purchase groceries.
- Aliases：store、shop、market

### So sánh giá
- Definition：Price comparison feature that analyzes ingredient costs across different stores to find the most economical shopping options for a given meal plan.
- Aliases：price comparison、cost comparison

### Chỉ đường
- Definition：Navigation feature that provides deep links to external mapping applications (Google Maps, Apple Maps) for directions to selected stores.
- Aliases：directions、navigation

### Crawl
- Definition：Automated web scraping process that collects product prices and availability from Vietnamese e-commerce sites on a scheduled basis.
- Aliases：scrape、data collection
